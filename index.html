Support Queue Doctor

A zero-dependency Python CLI that audits an open support queue snapshot CSV export for queue health problems: tickets older than 72 hours, unassigned old tickets, tickets stuck in pending or on-hold, first-response breaches, agent overload, and stale tickets with no activity. It also prints age buckets and per-agent and per-channel tables, so a support lead can see where the backlog sits and who carries it.
